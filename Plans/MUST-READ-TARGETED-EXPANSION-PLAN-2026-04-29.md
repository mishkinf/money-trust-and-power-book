## Expansion Principles

1. **Scene before theory.** Add paragraphs that put readers in a room, ledger, shop, field, mint, committee, or market before abstracting the mechanism.
2. **Research-backed specificity.** Every added factual claim must come from `Research/`, chapter `sources.md`, or `Content/07-Notes-on-Sources/01-main.md`.
3. **No repeated thesis explanation.** The book already states taxation, capacity, institutional trust, and paired vindication clearly. New words should dramatize or evidence those claims.
4. **Keep the Epilogue short.** The Epilogue now closes well. Do not rebuild the policy appendix.
5. **Full-read rule remains.** Before editing any chapter, read the full current chapter again, then its `sources.md`, then the research files listed below.
