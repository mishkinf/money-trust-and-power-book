## Current Safe Baseline

Assume the book's current safest posture is:

- 2021-22 inflation was a capacity-and-demand collision, not a simple money-printing morality tale.
- U.S. institutions prevented a runaway 1970s-style expectations spiral, but not painlessly or perfectly.
- Fed balance-sheet expansion after 2008/COVID did not mechanically translate into CPI inflation.
- Stablecoins mostly repackage dollar claims and Treasury/bank assets rather than escaping fiat.
- Crypto did not abolish institutional trust; it relocated trust into exchanges, custodians, stablecoin issuers, code governance, and legal bankruptcy regimes.

If new data complicates any of these claims, identify exactly how.
