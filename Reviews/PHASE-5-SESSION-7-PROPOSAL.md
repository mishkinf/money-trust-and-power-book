# Phase 5 Session 7 — Contemporary Hooks → Codas

Verified line-numbered proposals for relocating chapter-opening contemporary parallels to chapter codas, per `Reviews/REVISION-PLAN.md` ("Move chapter-opening contemporary hooks to codas; pick one parallel per chapter; eliminate mid-chapter intrusions").

The principle: every Part I–III chapter currently opens with a several-paragraph modern-day framing (Bitcoin, credit cards, the Greek crisis, 2008, March 2020, the gold-standard revival, household-budget rhetoric, Tsipras, the China Treasury position) before reaching the historical narrative scene. Repeated 11 times across the book, the device sets a recap-then-flashback rhythm that flattens narrative drive and makes every chapter feel like a thesis essay rather than a story. Moving the parallel to the coda lets the historical scene open the chapter with its full arresting force, and lets the modern echo land at the end as confirmation rather than throat-clearing.

The author has already directed (Phase 4) that strong narrative-arc hooks into the next chapter must be preserved. The codas proposed here do not displace those hooks — they sit before them.
