**Method:** four fresh blank-context reviewers, each instructed to read cover to cover and not rely on prior reviews, plans, or earlier conclusions.

## Executive Result

The manuscript is **near must-read with one narrow targeted pass remaining**.

No reviewer recommended another broad rewrite. All four agreed that the book now has a serious trade nonfiction spine, a strong thesis, memorable historical scenes, and a distinctive contribution in the paired-vindication frame. None said the book is fully 5/5 yet.

The consensus blockers are now narrow and convergent:

1. **Operational precision:** especially Chapter 12's Treasury-Fed / taxes-bonds-spending language.
2. **Causal modesty:** especially Churchill/coal/Todd, Weimar-to-Nazism, Volcker/oil shock, Japan/Kono, and Bretton Woods/bancor.
3. **Source anchoring:** especially Hagedorn, Todd, Lebesque, Kono, Terri Smith, and modern profile/reconstructed scenes.
4. **Final policy architecture:** the governance payoff is much improved, but reviewers still want a more concrete framework for what replaces household-budget thinking.
5. **Selective compression/cadence:** no bulk expansion; compress repeated institutional explanations and late Chapter 12 case pileup.

## Must-Read Diagnosis

The book is **not fully must-read yet**, but it is much closer than the prior state implied. The reviewers' consensus is that the architecture now works and the book should not be expanded into a longer, broader manuscript. The remaining gap is trust-hardening and final-payoff sharpening, not invention.

What would make it cross the line:

1. Tighten Chapter 12's operational accounting so Fed/Treasury mechanics are technically careful.
2. Add a compact policy/governance framework that gives readers a usable replacement for household-budget thinking.
3. Soften monocausal claims in the interwar, Bretton Woods, Volcker, Japan, and profile scenes.
4. Clean and strengthen the source apparatus for reconstructed human scenes and remove weak/placeholder citations.
5. Compress only where repetition remains obvious, especially early institutional recaps, Chapter 11 bridgework, and Chapter 12 case pileup.

What should not be touched:

- The broad four-part structure.
- Backwell as opening/closing figure.
- The paired-vindication frame.
- The Attwood-to-Churchill continuity.
- The refusal to write either gold nostalgia or fiat triumphalism.
- The human-scene method, as long as the source base is made visible.
