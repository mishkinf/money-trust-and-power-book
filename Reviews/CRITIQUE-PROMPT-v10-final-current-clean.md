## Reviewer Task

Evaluate the manuscript as a serious trade nonfiction book for intelligent general readers interested in money, power, history, inflation, debt, banking, crypto, and political economy.

## Required Output

1. **Native verdict**
   - Choose one:
     - 5/5 must-read, proceed out of substantive revision
     - 4.5/5 near must-read, only finishing cleanup remains
     - acquire / publish with revisions
     - competent but not must-read
     - not ready

2. **Go/no-go answer**
   - Has the manuscript crossed the must-read threshold?
   - Should the author stop substantive revision?
   - Are remaining issues blockers, or normal copyedit/source/page-number/proposal cleanup?

3. **Rubric scores**
   - Thesis force
   - Narrative momentum
   - Character and scene craft
   - Structural coherence
   - Prose quality
   - Originality / contribution
   - Scholarly/source credibility
   - Audience fit
   - Commercial/cultural potential
   - Faithfulness to credit-money/chartalist/source tradition where relevant
   - Policy clarity / practical payoff
   - Engagement curve

4. **Specific praise**
   - Name the strongest passages, scenes, arguments, and structural choices.

5. **Specific remaining concerns**
   - Do not give generic advice.
   - If something should be changed, name the chapter, passage/issue, and why.
   - Separate:
     - true substantive blockers
     - copyedit/cadence issues
     - source/page-number/fact-check issues
     - proposal/packaging issues

6. **Final recommendation**
   - One paragraph answering: if this crossed your desk today, would you call it must-read material, and what exactly should happen next?

## Important Calibration

Do not reward the manuscript for improvement trajectory. Judge only the current manuscript.

Do not punish the manuscript for lacking author-platform material; platform is out of scope here.

Do not ask for a larger book unless the manuscript truly lacks necessary substance. The current category target is a serious but readable trade nonfiction book, not an academic monograph.

Be candid. If it is not must-read, say so. If it is must-read but needs normal finishing work, say that clearly.
